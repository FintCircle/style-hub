# Style Hub

LeBeHo — MVP definition

LeBeHo is a fashion-exclusive social platform. People can use it primarily as a text/photo social network, primarily for short fashion videos, or both.

Feed is the main discovery-based social feed. It behaves somewhat like X: users can post text, photos, or text + photos. There are no videos in Feed. Posts can optionally include a vote, with two or multiple choices.

Instead of normal comments, the interaction is based around Seeker ↔ Stylist: the person posting is seeking opinions/advice, while other users contribute their fashion/style thoughts. There are no threaded replies underneath those responses.

A Feed post can optionally become Rush Hour. The creator chooses their own countdown duration. Active Rush Hour posts can be viewed separately by people who intentionally filter the Feed to Rush Hour, letting them find people who need fashion advice quickly. When time runs out, the urgency ends.

Reels are completely separate from Feed: fashion/style vertical videos up to 60 seconds. Their only social interaction for MVP is Like. No voting and no Thoughts.

Profiles bring the person's activity together, while the main Feed remains discovery-based rather than follower-driven.

So the product has a very understandable structure:

Feed · Rush Hour · Reels · Create · Profile

And importantly, LeBeHo isn't exclusively “help me choose my outfit.” Someone can post a fashion opinion, show what they're wearing, discuss a trend, ask a question, run a vote, seek advice, or simply talk fashion. Fashion is the constraint; the community decides how to use it.
